  1. The customer submits the request form and proforma (a scanned form can be read automatically).
  2. The Test In-charge accepts the sample; the sample code, test report number and work instruction are issued.
  3. Engineers enter readings from Excel logsheets; calculations and IS 1180 rule checks run on every save.
  4. The Test In-charge reviews every value, correction and the filled report, then forwards it to the HOD.
  5. The HOD authorizes the report. It is issued with a SHA-256 seal and the data can no longer be changed.
